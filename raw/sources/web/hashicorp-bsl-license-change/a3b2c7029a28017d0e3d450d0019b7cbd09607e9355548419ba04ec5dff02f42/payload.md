# HashiCorp — adoption of the Business Source License

- Source: https://www.hashicorp.com/en/blog/hashicorp-adopts-business-source-license
- Retrieved: 2026-09-13
- Announced: 2023-08-10
- Extracted for: whether the license change made the product paid, or restricted a
  specific class of use.

## Change

Previous license: Mozilla Public License v2.0 (MPL 2.0).
New license: Business Source License (BSL) v1.1, announced 2023-08-10.

## Who is unaffected

> End users can continue to copy, modify, and redistribute the code for all non-commercial
> and commercial use, except where providing a competitive offering.

> Partners can continue to build integrations for our joint customers.

> Customers of enterprise and cloud-managed HashiCorp products will see no change.

## What is restricted

> Vendors who provide competitive services built on our community products will no longer
> be able to incorporate future releases, bug fixes, or security patches contributed to
> our products.
